Locomotion Selects for Symmetry: Evolutionary Co-Design of Legged Robots on an Icosahedral Body

Jiong Lin, Yue Yao, Hod Lipson — Creative Machines Lab, Columbia University

IROS 2026 Workshop on Learning-based Robot Co-design · Code & figures

Legged robots on a regular icosahedron are co-designed — morphology by evolution, control by reinforcement learning — with fitness awarded only for displacement in any direction. Symmetry is scored exactly against the icosahedron's 15 reflection planes and compared with a drift baseline. In an abstract simulator populations become more symmetric than drift; in a simulator built from the fabricable robot's CAD the effect shrinks by an order of magnitude and no longer predicts the winner.
